<h2 align="center">🚀 Featured Automation Projects</h2>

### Lead Capture & Instant Notifications

- Captures a lead from a website form and records the lead information.
- Sends the business owner an email containing the hot lead’s information.
- Sends a Telegram notification so the owner can receive the alert on their phone.
- Helps the owner respond quickly to new opportunities.

### Automated Invoicing

- Receives invoice information submitted by an employee.
- Processes the data using spreadsheet calculations.
- Generates a professional PDF invoice.
- Emails the invoice directly to the customer.
- Records the processing status for tracking.

### E‑commerce Orders & Inventory

- Receives structured order information (compatible with Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce).
- Records the order and updates inventory quantities.
- Sends a notification to the fulfillment/warehouse team.
- Sends an order confirmation to the customer.
- Detects low-stock conditions and sends a Telegram alert when inventory falls below a configured threshold.
