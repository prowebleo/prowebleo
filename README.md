## 🚀 Featured Automation Projects

### 01 — Lead Capture & Instant Notifications

**Capture leads, organize their information, and notify the business instantly.**

This workflow demonstrates how a new lead can be captured from a website form, recorded automatically, and immediately delivered to the business owner through email and Telegram notifications.

**Key capabilities:**

* Lead capture
* Data organization
* Automated email notifications
* Telegram alerts
* Real-time lead follow-up

### 02 — Automated Invoicing

**Connect invoice data with document generation, storage, and delivery.**

This workflow takes invoice information submitted by an employee, records and processes the data, generates a professional invoice, stores the resulting document, and sends it directly to the customer by email.

**Key capabilities:**

* Invoice data capture
* Automated calculations
* Document generation
* PDF creation and storage
* Automated email delivery
* Status tracking

### 03 — E-commerce Orders & Inventory

**Connect incoming orders with inventory management and operational notifications.**

This workflow processes incoming order data, records the order, updates inventory according to the quantities sold, notifies the fulfillment team, confirms the order with the customer, and generates low-stock alerts when necessary.

**Key capabilities:**

* E-commerce order processing
* Inventory updates
* Stock threshold monitoring
* Fulfillment notifications
* Customer email confirmations
* Low-stock Telegram alerts
